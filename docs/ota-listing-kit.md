## Rules before you list

1. Use the **same trading name** everywhere: “Bahari Asili Safaris”, Watamu, Kenya, +254 101 923 355, bahariasilisafaris@gmail.com. Different names on different OTAs split the entity and weaken every AI answer.
2. Use the **50-word description and included/excluded lists exactly as written**. Do not add prices, awards, certifications, “best in Kenya” claims or review counts that the business cannot evidence.
3. Publish a **cancellation policy** first (currently TODO). Every OTA requires one, and it must match the website.
4. Do **not** promise wildlife sightings, migration dates or weather. The copy deliberately says “possible”, “usually” and “not guaranteed”.
5. Keep the **price basis** consistent: quoted per trip, with high/low season noted. When a reviewed from-price is approved, add it here and on the site in the same week.
6. Re-check every listing at least twice a year and whenever this file is regenerated.

## Review collection flow

Reviews are the strongest trust signal for AI answers, but only real, first-party reviews may be shown. Recommended flow:

1. On the last morning, the guide asks whether the guest is willing to review and sends the direct Google or TripAdvisor link by WhatsApp.
2. The booking system sends one follow-up message 48 hours after the trip ends (existing email/WhatsApp pipeline) with the same link. One reminder only.
3. Never offer a discount, upgrade or gift in exchange for a review.
4. When reviews exist, add them to the program page with a visible source and then — and only then — add `AggregateRating` to the structured data.

The 2-Day Tsavo East Safari is a 2-day, 1-night private safari from Watamu visiting Tsavo East. It suits couples, families and small groups, with Red-dust elephants and Mudanda Rock viewpoint among the highlights. Prices are quoted per trip. The most reliable months are June, July, August, when Tsavo and southern Kenya is usually dry.

**Included**

- KWS park entry fees
- 4x4 safari vehicle with pop-up roof

- Full board (breakfast, lunch, dinner)
- Drinking water during game drives
- All accommodation as per itinerary
- Airport / hotel pickup & drop-off

**Not included**

- International & domestic flights
- Visa fees
- Travel & medical insurance
- Tips and gratuities for guides & staff
- Alcoholic & premium bottled drinks
- Personal expenses & souvenirs
- Optional activities (balloon safaris, spa, etc.)

**Best time**

For the 2-Day Tsavo East Safari, the strongest months are June, July, August, September and October, then January, February, March and December, when Tsavo and southern Kenya is normally dry and wildlife concentrates near water. April is the least reliable because of the long or short rains. Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed, and rain dates shift from year to year.

**Packing (first ten items)**

- High SPF sunscreen (50+)
- Tropical insect repellent (DEET-based)
- Wide-brim hat
- Light long-sleeved shirts and trousers (neutral colours)
- Swimwear and flip-flops for lodge pool
- Warm fleece or light down layer for early-morning game drives
- English 3-pin plug adapter (Type G)
- Binoculars (7x50 or 10x42)
- Small torch/headlamp
- Camera with spare battery

**Health and safety**

Kenya’s game parks carry malaria risk, so the CDC advises prescription prophylaxis for this trip, and yellow-fever vaccination is recommended for Tsavo, the Mara and Nakuru. Travel insurance with medical evacuation is strongly advised. This is general guidance, not medical advice — confirm vaccinations, medication and entry rules with a travel doctor and the official Kenya eTA portal before you travel.
