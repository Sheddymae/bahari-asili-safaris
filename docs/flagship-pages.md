## Required owner-verified fields before a page is marked complete

For each selected programme or excursion, confirm:

- Exact route and day-by-day timing, including approximate driving hours for each safari day.
- Inclusions and exclusions for that specific product.
- Pickup areas actually served and the exact meeting point.
- Vehicle type, passenger capacity and maximum group size.
- Minimum age and child-seat availability, or an explicit “ask us” policy.
- Cancellation and deposit policy, consistent with the booking terms and OTA listings.
- Approved price, currency and price basis; until approved, keep “quoted per trip” and do not emit an Offer.
- Best months and seasonal caveats for the actual parks or marine activity.
- Family and older-traveller suitability grounded in drive times and access details.
- Packing and health guidance with current official sources.
- Five or six distinct FAQs that do not simply repeat the visible page sections.
- Real product photos with accurate, descriptive alt text.
- A human-confirmed `lastReviewedAt` date; do not use the build date or a shared placeholder date.
